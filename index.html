Honest by design

Private. Zero product selling. Clear limits.

Built on the conviction that personal finance education should have zero conflicts of interest.

What KYF is

  • A 100% educational simulator and personal finance calculator designed to give you intuition and clarity.
  • Pocket-Calculator Mode: runs strictly in your browser (IndexedDB / localStorage) with zero tracking cookies or remote servers.
  • Equipped with a 1-click portable .kyf vault save/open engine and an instant data wipe reset.
  • Built to empower you to have sharper, confident conversations with fee-only SEBI-registered advisors and your family.

What it is not

  • Not investment, tax, or legal advice, and not a SEBI-registered advisory service or brokerage.
  • Never sells insurance, mutual funds, loans, credit cards, or demat accounts—strictly zero affiliate links or distributor kickbacks.
  • Historical index, stock, and mutual fund benchmarks shown are strictly for educational illustration, never guarantees of future returns.
