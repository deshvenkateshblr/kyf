How it works

Four steps to make your money work for you.

  1. 1

    Bring it all together

    Add stocks, funds, FDs, gold, property, EPF and PPF, and your loans, from every bank and broker, for the whole family.

    You get: one honest net worth, instead of five apps and a spreadsheet.

  2. 2

    Name your goals

    Home, college, retirement, insurance, or your own.

    You get: each goal priced in future rupees, with the monthly amount it needs.

  3. 3

    Give money a job

    Match what you own to each goal.

    You get: to see which goals are funded, and which money is sitting idle.

  4. 4

    Stay on track

    A checklist and dates for every goal, plus the yearly chores that protect your family.

    You get: a habit that turns plans into progress, with a nudge when something is due.

Your data

All of it lives in one file, on your device.

Private by design

  • Save your whole profile as a single .kyf file. Open it on any device to carry on.
  • No account, no server, no tracking. Nothing leaves your browser unless you share a file or link.
  • Keep a copy of the file somewhere safe. Nobody else has one, so we cannot recover it for you.

Education, not advice

  • Not investment, tax or legal advice, and not a registered advisor.
  • We never sell insurance, funds or loans. No affiliate links.
