1. Real Inflation Trap

CPI headline inflation is ~5.4%, but Higher Education compounds at 9–10% and Healthcare at 8–11%. KYF applies sector-specific compounding so you aren't surprised a decade later.

2. Timeline → Asset Recipe

Under 3 years: 100% Debt/Liquid. Never risk equity for short-term goals. 3–7 years: 40% Equity / 50% Debt. Over 7 years: 70% Equity / 20% Debt / 10% Gold for long-term compounding.

3. Step-Up Compounding

Increasing your SIP by just 10% each year as your salary grows cuts your required starting investment almost in half while protecting your monthly peace of mind.
