💡 The KYF Cadence Principle on Market Performance

The goal of reviewing market performance in your cadence is not to chase the top gainer of the last month or panic over short-term drawdowns. It is to verify whether your long-term return assumptions remain grounded, inspect whether equity valuations are demanding or cheap, and rebalance asset allocations calmly according to plan.
