Understanding Property Pricing Reality

Key principles every residential homebuyer must evaluate before signing an agreement to sell.

📐

RERA Carpet Area Standard

Under Section 2(k) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act 2016, Carpet Area is the net usable floor area of an apartment, excluding the area covered by external walls and balcony. By law, builders must disclose carpet area.

🏢

Super Built-Up Area (SBUA) Loading

Builders calculate Super Built-Up area by adding common spaces (elevators, staircases, generator rooms, corridors, and clubhouses). A 35% loading means that out of every 1,350 sq.ft. billed, 350 sq.ft. is external shared infrastructure.

⚖️

The Headline Rate Mirage

A builder quoting ₹8,000/sq.ft. with 40% loading has a true livable carpet rate of ₹11,200/sq.ft. That is more expensive than a competitor quoting ₹9,000/sq.ft. with 20% loading (₹10,800/sq.ft. carpet area).

🏛️

Sunk Statutory Friction

Stamp Duty (5% to 7% state-dependent) and Registration (1%) are non-recoverable transaction friction. On a ₹1.5 Cr agreement, over ₹10 Lakhs is paid upfront before home loan disbursement or interior fitout expenses.
